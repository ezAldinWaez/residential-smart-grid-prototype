    Thanks and Appreciation
    =======================

We extend our deepest gratitude to all those who supported and guided us throughout this journey, making the Residential Smart Grid Prototype a meaningful and enriching experience.

First and foremost, we express our sincere appreciation to **Dr. Fadi Farha**, our project supervisor, whose invaluable guidance, expertise, and unwavering support made this project possible. 

We are grateful to the **Faculty of Informatics Engineering** at the **University of Aleppo** for providing the academic environment for conducting this research. 

Our appreciation extends to the faculty members of the **Department of Systems and Computer Networks** who contributed to our education that enabled us to understand and implement system architecture principles that form the backbone of this project.

We acknowledge the invaluable contributions of open-source communities and research institutions whose tools and datasets made this work possible. Special recognition goes to the developers of **pvlib**, **pvwatts**, and the **National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)**. We thank the **National Solar Radiation Database (NSRDB)** team for making comprehensive solar irradiance data freely available for research purposes.
